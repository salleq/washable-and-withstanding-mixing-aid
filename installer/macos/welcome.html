WWMA – Washable and Withstanding Mixing Aid

Tells you, in plain words, what is too much or too little in your mix, and teaches you how to fix it yourself.

This installs the VST3 plugin and the Audio Unit for all users. Rescan plugins in your DAW afterwards, and put WWMA last on your master bus.

Your sound passes through untouched, except when you listen to a reference track with the A/B switch or turn on WWMA's own mastering EQ or limiter (both off by default). WWMA only suggests settings: you turn the knobs.

Free and open source: github.com/salleq/washable-and-withstanding-mixing-aid
